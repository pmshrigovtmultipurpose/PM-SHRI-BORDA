A Message from the Headmistress

A school is not just a building; it is a place where dreams take shape, values are nurtured and futures are created. As we step into our new premises, we begin a new chapter filled with hope, responsibility and endless possibilities.

At PM SHRI Government Multipurpose High School, we believe that every child is unique, with individual talents, dreams and aspirations. As a Multipurpose School, we strive to cater to the diverse needs and interests of every learner, providing opportunities to grow and excel in their chosen areas. With our dedicated and capable faculty, we are committed to nurturing academic excellence along with sports, co-curricular activities, creativity, innovation, leadership and life skills.

We believe that true education goes beyond the four walls of the classroom. Our strong bonds with parents, alumni and society at large help us create a supportive and enriching environment where our students learn to become responsible, confident and compassionate citizens.

With the dedication of our teachers and the support of our entire school community, we are committed to building a school that our students will always be proud to call their own.

Let us preserve what we have, nurture what we build and work together to create a legacy that will inspire generations to come since 1953.

“Together, we learn. Together, we grow. Together, we build the future.”
SONAM MAHADEV NAIK Headmistress

Admissions 2026–27

Join Our School Family

Admissions are open for Std 5 to 10 for the academic year 2026–2027. Free uniform, shoes, and books provided to all students.

1

Collect Form

Visit the school office at Borda, Margao or call us to get the admission form.

2

Submit Documents

Submit form with birth certificate, previous school report card, and passport photo.

3

Verification

Documents will be verified by administration within 2–3 working days.

4

Enroll & Start!

Receive your confirmation, free uniform, shoes, and books. Welcome to PM SHRI!

🏅 NSQF Certified School — Retail & IT Vocational Courses

We offer Industry-Aligned Career Subjects under NSQF (National Skills Qualifications Framework) in Retail and IT — giving our students a head start in the job market right from school.
